# Runledge

**Runledge is the approval gate for AI agents.** Rules check every action before it runs; risky actions are Held for human approval, and every decision is recorded in a signed ledger.

Stripe is infrastructure for payments. Runledge is risk infrastructure for agent actions.

## One ledger. Three products.

- **The Gate** — writes the ledger. Every agent action is checked against your rules before it runs: Allowed, Adjusted, Held, or Blocked. Approvals happen where your team already works — Slack, Teams, or email.
- **Runledge AI** — reads the ledger. Reviews your agents' recorded activity, finds the patterns and gaps your rules don't cover, and proposes new rules. It proposes; you decide.
- **The Clearinghouse** — shares the ledger. Signed receipts anyone can verify, so an approval or a decision can be proven across company lines.
